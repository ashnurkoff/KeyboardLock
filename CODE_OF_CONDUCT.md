# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information.
- Make space for newcomers and different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading reports.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an urgent or private conduct concern, use [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/ashnurkoff/KeyboardLock/security/advisories/new) and label the report as a code-of-conduct matter. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.

No one who is directly involved in a complaint should make the final enforcement decision alone when another maintainer is available.
